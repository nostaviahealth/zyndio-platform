## Important Compliance Notes

> [!WARNING]
> **Health Claims**: Avoid any language suggesting the app can diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Use terms like "insights," "tracking," "preventive health," and "wellness optimization."

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Medical Disclaimers**: Ensure all screenshots showing health data or risk predictions include visible disclaimers or at least mention them in screenshot captions.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Before/After Claims**: Never use testimonials or before/after transformations in health apps—this is explicitly prohibited by both app stores.
